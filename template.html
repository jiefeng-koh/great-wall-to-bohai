Beijing: arrival and the Great Wall

Sleep: Kerry Hotel Beijing, Chaoyang · 13–15 June

Day1
Sunday 13 JuneToday

Arrive in Chaoyang

Nothing booked, and dinner is a 10-minute walk, so a late landing costs nothing.

{{fig:hotpot|Copper chimney hotpot with plates of sliced mutton|Copper-pot hotpot, Beijing style}}{{fig:china-world|China World towers and hotel at Guomao, Beijing|China World, next to the Kerry}}{{fig:cctv-tower|The looped CCTV Headquarters tower against a blue sky|The CCTV tower, seen from the mall terrace}}
  1. Arrive

    Check in at the Kerry 北京嘉里大酒店

    Let the kids loose in the hotel's adventure zone and pool.

    {{go:北京嘉里大酒店|Kerry Hotel Beijing}}
  2. Dinner

    Nanmen Shuanrou, China World Mall 南门涮肉 国贸商城店

    Beijing copper-pot hotpot in clear broth, so nothing spicy for the kids. Order the hand-cut lamb (手切鲜羊肉), beef 上脑, the house sesame sauce and the homemade 烧饼. It queues hard: phone about 2 hours ahead for a number (it rolls over three tables if you're late), or be there by 17:00.

    {{go:南门涮肉 国贸商城店|Nanmen Shuanrou, China World Mall}}
  3. After

    China World Mall 国贸商城

    LEGO, FAO Schwarz, the Sisyphe bookshop's big children's corner (North B2), the ice rink, and a free view of the CCTV tower from the 6F terrace.Xiaohongshu: “遛娃天花板”

    {{go:国贸商城|China World Mall}}

Nearby & plan B

  • Queue too longPinglu sushi train 平禄回转寿司North 5F of the same mall. A little train brings the plates, ¥10–15 each. Kids love it; the food is just OK.{{go:平禄回转寿司 国贸商城|Pinglu Kaiten Sushi, China World Mall}}
  • Landing lateKerry's Kitchen buffet, inside the Kerry 嘉品餐厅The package includes the kids' play area, and there's a clown show on weekends.{{go:北京嘉里大酒店|Kerry Hotel Beijing}}

Day2
Monday 14 JuneToday

Mutianyu Great Wall

Monday is the quietest day on the Wall, and the Forbidden City is closed anyway. About 1.5 hours by car each way.

{{fig:mutianyu-cablecar|Orange Mutianyu cable cars over green wooded hills|The cable car up}}{{fig:mutianyu-tower|A watchtower on the Mutianyu wall among spring trees|A Mutianyu watchtower}}{{fig:toboggan|The toboggan track running down through the trees from the Wall|The toboggan down}}{{fig:lamb-skewers|Lamb skewers smoking on a charcoal grill|Lamb skewers for dinner}}
  1. Breakfast

    Hotel buffet at the Kerry

    Early start, so keep it easy.

    {{go:北京嘉里大酒店|Kerry Hotel Beijing}}
  2. 08:00

    Mutianyu Great Wall 慕田峪长城

    Leave by 8:00. Cable car up, toboggan down. On a Monday morning there's hardly a queue. Children from 1.2 m ride with an adult, one child per adult.

    {{go:慕田峪长城|Mutianyu Great Wall}}
  3. Lunch

    Rainbow trout in Mutianyu village

    The village speciality, grilled or braised. No single place stands out on Xiaohongshu, so pick any busy one.

    {{go:慕田峪村|Mutianyu village}}
  4. Rest stop

    Mu·Cafe at the foot of the Wall

    Great Wall-themed cups and a view of the watchtowers. Mutianyu is chestnut country, so ask for anything chestnut.

    {{go:慕田峪长城|Mutianyu Great Wall}}
  5. 15:00

    Back at the Kerry

    Nap and pool.

    {{go:北京嘉里大酒店|Kerry Hotel Beijing}}
  6. Late p.m.

    Hopson One 朝阳合生汇

    About 10 minutes by taxi. The mall young Beijingers use: trend and toy shops, snacks on every floor, and a three-level kids' play area. Pick up Lingcha or Yeye Bu Pao Cha milk tea.

    {{go:朝阳合生汇|Hopson One, Chaoyang}}
  7. 17:00

    Hen Jiu Yi Qian, Hopson One branch 很久以前羊肉串 合生汇店

    The lamb-skewer chain, inside the mall you're already in. A Monday at 17:00 is about as quiet as it gets; if there's a wait, take a number on Dianping and go back to the toy shops. Skip the cold noodles.Xiaohongshu: service “比海底捞服务好”, a little pricey

    {{go:很久以前羊肉串 合生汇店|Hen Jiu Yi Qian, Hopson One}}

Nearby & plan B

  • Heavy rain on the WallSwap Day 2 and stay in the cityThe Wall is slippery and the toboggan closes in rain. Spend the day at Hopson One and the Kerry pool, and try the Wall another morning if a day frees up.
  • Lunch in the villageThe Schoolhouse at MutianyuA long-running restaurant in a converted village school, with Western and Chinese dishes.{{go:慕田峪村|Mutianyu village}}
  • Skewers queue too longZi Guang Yuan 紫光园Beijing halal chain: lamb and beef, no chilli. Pick the nearest branch.{{go:紫光园|Zi Guang Yuan}}

Days 3–4

Datong: walls, grottoes and palace nights

Sleep: Sunyata by Jixiashan, old city · 15–17 June. Big bags go to the Ascott on the way.

Day3
Tuesday 15 JuneToday

Old city, a wall ride and a show

About two hours by high-speed train. The evening runs to about 21:30, so keep the afternoon easy.

{{fig:shaomai|A steamer of thin-skinned mutton-and-scallion shaomai|Thin-skinned shaomai}}{{fig:huayan|A hall at Huayan Temple with a bare tree in the courtyard|Huayan Temple}}{{fig:nine-dragon|Glazed tile dragons on Datong's Nine-Dragon Wall|The Nine-Dragon Wall, glazed in 1392}}{{fig:datong-oldcity|Carved wooden Sipailou arches on Yongtai Street, Datong old city|Sipailou arches, old-city crossroads}}{{fig:datong-wall-tower|A corner tower on Datong's restored Ming city wall|The wall you ride at sunset}}{{fig:datong-south-gate|Datong city wall South Gate (Yongtai Gate) tower in daytime|The South Gate, where the ride and the show end}}
  1. Breakfast

    Qingfeng Baozi 庆丰包子铺

    About 7:30. Beijing's classic steamed-bun chain, branches all over the CBD. Pork-and-scallion buns for everyone.

    {{go:庆丰包子铺|Qingfeng Steamed Buns}}
  2. Bags

    Drop the big bags at the Ascott

    Taxi from the Kerry to the Ascott at Dongzhimen and leave the big bags (ask the hotel in writing beforehand to hold them for two nights), then on to Beijing North. It's on the way north, so it adds about 10 minutes.

    {{go:北京雅诗阁来福士中心服务公寓|Ascott Raffles City Beijing}}
  3. Train

    Beijing North or Qinghe → Datong South, about 2 h

    Aim for one around 9:30–10:00. Then about 25 minutes by taxi to Sunyata.

    {{go:大同南站|Datong South Station}}
  4. 12:30

    Longjuxiang Shaomai 龙聚祥烧麦

    By the Drum Tower. Lamb shaomai with no gamey taste. Queues of up to 30 minutes; skip the fried-chicken stall opposite.Xiaohongshu: “烧麦界的天花板”

    {{go:大同龙聚祥|Longjuxiang Shaomai, Datong}}
  5. 13:30

    Huayan Temple 华严寺 and the Nine-Dragon Wall 九龙壁

    Both in the old city, a short walk apart.

    {{go:大同华严寺|Huayan Temple, Datong}}{{go:大同九龙壁|Nine-Dragon Wall, Datong}}
  6. 15:15

    Dongnanyi quarter 东南邑

    Restored lanes with craft studios and small galleries; free street shows (stilts, lion dance, magic) from 16:00 in summer.

    {{go:大同东南邑|Dongnanyi, Datong}}
  7. Rest stop

    Mulan ice cream 木兰冰淇淋

    The matcha beats Wuyutai's in Beijing, say Datong locals. A copy sits right opposite, so check the name. Or sea-buckthorn juice from any stall.

    {{go:大同木兰冰淇淋|Mulan Ice Cream, Datong}}
  8. 17:30

    Kaige, Dongnanyi branch 凯鸽酒楼 东南邑店

    Inside Dongnanyi, so no taxi. Order 风沙鸡 (crisp chicken), 风味茄子 (eggplant), 大骨头 (lamb bones, ¥7 each) and 过油肉; half portions available.Xiaohongshu: “我为你举大旗”

    {{go:凯鸽酒楼 东南邑店|Kaige Restaurant, Dongnanyi}}
  9. 19:00

    City wall from the East Gate 和阳门

    150 m from Sunyata. Take the sightseeing cart (¥40 a person) or a tandem (¥100): parents say the six-seat bikes are hard to pedal and small children bounce. 90-minute limit, ¥50 deposit. Ride south, then west to the South Gate, for sunset about 20:00. Check the last rental time on the day.

    {{go:大同古城墙和阳门|Datong city wall, East Gate}}
  10. 20:00

    《因为大同・北魏城中城》, South Gate 永泰门

    About 90 minutes, ¥79: a walk-through show with lots of actors talking to the audience. Taxi back, about 5 minutes.Xiaohongshu: “好玩”

    {{go:大同古城墙永泰门|Datong city wall, South Gate}}

Nearby & plan B

  • Hot afternoon or rainDatong Museum 大同市博物馆Dinosaur fossils and Northern Wei treasures, indoors. Open Tuesday.{{go:大同市博物馆|Datong Museum}}
  • Too tired for the showFree 3D light shows 鼓楼 钟楼 四牌楼On the Drum Tower (a Mulan story), Bell Tower and Sipailou at 19:00, 20:00 and 21:00, 5–10 minutes each.{{go:大同鼓楼|Datong Drum Tower}}
  • Kaige fullZini 369, Sipailou 紫泥369 四牌楼店Polarising on Xiaohongshu; go for the 沙葱莜面 and cheese cake.{{go:紫泥369 四牌楼店|Zini 369, Sipailou}}

Day4
Wednesday 16 JuneToday

Yungang Grottoes and a palace night

An early start, a long midday rest, and a late night for the molten-iron sparks. Tomorrow starts slowly.

{{fig:yungang|The great seated Buddha of Cave 20 at Yungang|Cave 20, the Great Buddha}}{{fig:yungang-buddha|A painted Buddha inside a Yungang cave|Colour still survives inside}}{{fig:hanfu|Two women in hanfu at a red temple door|Dressing up in hanfu}}{{fig:daiwangfu|Glazed dragon screen and stone lion at the gate of Daiwangfu, Datong|Daiwangfu, the Ming prince's palace}}{{fig:datiehua|Molten-iron sparks bursting under temple eaves at night|Molten-iron sparks, the 22:35 finale}}
  1. Early

    Breakfast at Sunyata

    Ask the night before for an early breakfast or a box. Yungang is about 30 minutes by car.

    {{go:大同既下山酒店|Sunyata by Jixiashan, Datong}}
  2. Opening

    Yungang Grottoes 云冈石窟

    Opening time is given as 7:00–8:30; confirm when you book on the official WeChat account. Walk it in reverse, Cave 20 to Cave 5, to stay ahead of the groups. Kid hunt: the V-sign Buddha in Cave 18 and the high-five Buddha in Cave 19. Rent the audio guide.Xiaohongshu: “反向游览yyds”

    {{go:云冈石窟|Yungang Grottoes}}
  3. 11:30

    Tongle Lou hotpot 同乐楼火锅

    Opposite Huayan Temple: copper-pot lamb hotpot, one small pot each.

    {{go:大同同乐楼火锅|Tongle Lou Hotpot, Datong}}
  4. Rest stop

    Afternoon at the hotel

    Snack: fried yellow-millet cakes (油炸糕) from an old-city stall.

    {{go:大同既下山酒店|Sunyata by Jixiashan, Datong}}
  5. 16:30

    Tianyuan Beiwei Jiayan 田园北魏家宴 大都府店

    About 10 minutes by taxi. Locals' pick, no queue: 平城烤鸡 (roast chicken), 胡饼 (flatbread), 浑源凉粉 (cold jelly noodles).

    {{go:田园北魏家宴 大都府店|Tianyuan Beiwei Jiayan}}
  6. 17:30

    Daiwangfu, hanfu at golden hour 代王府

    Buy the performance ticket (entry 14:00–22:00). Costume photos 18:30–19:15. Lantern hire ¥20 plus ¥50 deposit. Bring only small bags: the bag store closes before the shows end.

    {{go:大同代王府|Daiwangfu, Datong}}
  7. 19:30

    Tianxia Datong, then the sparks

    Find a spot early. 21:10 nuo masked dance, 22:20 lanterns, 22:35 molten-iron sparks (打铁花). Pre-book a car for about 22:50; taxis are scarce after the show. Back about 23:15.

    {{go:大同代王府|Daiwangfu, Datong}}

Nearby & plan B

  • A bigger day outHanging Temple 悬空寺A temple pinned to a cliff face, about 1.5 hours by car. Only if you swap out the afternoon rest.{{go:悬空寺|Hanging Temple}}
  • Kids done with templesDatong Museum 大同市博物馆Dinosaur hall, indoors. Summer last entry 18:30.{{go:大同市博物馆|Datong Museum}}
  • Too late for the sparksLeave after Tianxia DatongBack by about 21:00 and a normal bedtime.

Day5
Thursday 17 JuneToday

Back to Beijing, into the quest world

A slow morning after the late night. The bags are already at the Ascott, so a taxi does.

{{fig:jiaozi|Plates of jiaozi dumplings|Dumplings for dinner}}{{fig:ludagun|Plate of lüdagun sticky rice rolls coated in soybean flour|Donkey rolls (lüdagun) at Huguosi}}
  1. 09:00

    Laochai Xiaomian, main shop 老柴削面 总店

    Corner of Yongtai South Road and Xiangyang Street. Knife-cut noodles, ¥11. Ask for 不要辣 (no chilli) for the kids. Skip the old-city shop with the same name; it may be a copy.

    {{go:大同老柴削面总店|Laochai Knife-cut Noodles, main shop}}
  2. Train

    Datong → Beijing, about 2 h

    A train around 11:00–11:30, arriving Qinghe or Beijing North about 13:00. Taxi to Beitou.

    {{go:大同南站|Datong South Station}}
  3. Lunch

    Feifu Paidang, Beitou 肥福排档

    Cantonese, in the same mall: the roast goose is the one to order, and nothing is spicy. For a snack on the way out, 鲍师傅 (Bao Shifu) pastries: the seaweed-and-pork-floss sponge is the bestseller. Skip Goat 美式小馆 (chaotic service in a 2025 review).Xiaohongshu: roast goose “必推” (must order)

    {{go:肥福排档 北投购物公园|Feifu Paidang, Beitou Shopping Park}}
  4. 14:00

    Imaginary World, Beitou 一起幻城 北投店

    The one you go into through a giant toilet bowl. 4,000 m² on two floors: wristband quests through ancient-China and space worlds. Plan 3–4 hours; you can leave to eat and come back. Bring non-slip socks.

    {{go:北投购物公园|Beitou Shopping Park}}
  5. Rest stop

    Huguosi Snacks, Di'anmen 护国寺小吃 地安门店

    On the way south, until 20:30: pea cake (豌豆黄), donkey rolls (驴打滚) and naipizi milk-skin yogurt.

    {{go:护国寺小吃地安门店|Huguosi Snacks, Di'anmen}}
  6. On the way

    Daoxiangcun Treasure-Hunt Store, Longfu Temple 稻香村 隆福寺

    Beijing pastries in novelty boxes. The last stop before the hotel.

    {{go:隆福寺稻香村|Daoxiangcun, Longfu Temple}}
  7. Dinner

    Xianlaoman, Andingmen 馅老满 安定门店

    A classic Beijing dumpling house, on the way from Beitou to the Ascott. Order the signature 老满饺子 (pork, chives, egg and whole shrimp), the vegetarian 素老满, and 赛螃蟹 (egg scrambled with vinegar and ginger to taste like crab). Dumplings are sold from 2 liang, about 10 each. Then the first load in the washer-dryer.Xiaohongshu: “皮薄馅大” (thin skin, big filling), 1,212 likes, Aug 2026

    {{go:馅老满 安定门店|Xianlaoman Dumplings, Andingmen}}

Day6
Friday 18 JuneToday

The Forbidden City

Walk from the hotel. You enter from the south (Meridian Gate) and leave by the north, so the day flows one way. Bring passports: tickets are real-name.

{{fig:meridian-gate|The Meridian Gate of the Forbidden City across the stone forecourt|In by the Meridian Gate}}{{fig:kunning-palace|Palace of Earthly Tranquility in the Forbidden City|Kunning Palace, where tea is served}}{{fig:jingshan-view|The golden roofs of the Forbidden City seen from Jingshan hill|The view from Jingshan}}{{fig:wangfujing|Crowds walking down the Wangfujing pedestrian street|Down Wangfujing to Xiyue}}
  1. Breakfast

    At the Ascott

    Check whether your rate includes breakfast. The palace opens at 8:30: leave by 7:50 and take a taxi to Tiananmen East (天安门东, about 20 minutes), then walk in to the Meridian Gate.

    {{go:北京雅诗阁来福士中心服务公寓|Ascott Raffles City Beijing}}
  2. 08:30

    Forbidden City, Meridian Gate 故宫博物院 午门

    South to north. On the east side, 从故宫到天宫 at Jianting Square is a space-themed shop with a full-size space-station tea bar.

    {{go:故宫博物院午门|Forbidden City, Meridian Gate}}
  3. Lunch

    Inside the palace

    The restaurant by the Clock and Treasure galleries. Skip the Ice House restaurant: food is pre-made and pricey.

    {{go:故宫博物院|Palace Museum}}
  4. Rest stop

    Kunning Palace afternoon tea 万春金福

    In the east court of Kunning Palace, on the way north. Take a number on site from 9:30, or on Dianping after 11:30 (萬春金福下午茶 故宫店). Order the plum-blossom 妃子饮, the 万春金福 latte and the persimmon cake; about ¥65 a head.Xiaohongshu: better than the Corner Tower Café, “只有颜值”

    {{go:萬春金福下午茶 故宫店|Wanchun Jinfu tea, Palace Museum}}
  5. Then

    Jingshan Park 景山公园

    Just outside the north exit. Climb for the view over the palace roofs, then a taxi back to the Ascott to rest (about 20 minutes).

    {{go:景山公园|Jingshan Park}}
  6. Late p.m.

    Wangfujing Xiyue, B2 王府井喜悦

    JUMP and Animate stores and rotating pop-ups. Wuyutai jasmine-tea ice cream on the way.Xiaohongshu: “北京二次元浓度最高地”

    {{go:王府井喜悦|Wangfujing Xiyue}}
  7. Dinner

    Guijie 簋街

    The restaurant street 3 minutes from the Ascott, where Beijingers go for crayfish and hotpot at night. Ask for garlic crayfish (蒜蓉) for the kids rather than the spicy kind, and take a queue number online.

    {{go:簋街|Guijie, Dongzhimen}}
  8. Night

    Last wash, then pack day bags

    Four nights of clothes and swimwear for Universal and the sea. The big cases stay at the Ascott until you're back on 23 June.

Galleries open about 10:00 and most close on Mondays, so Saturday works. Check out first and leave the big cases with the front desk; book a car for the day to carry the day bags.

    {{go:增盛魁|Zengshengkui}}

    798 Art District 798艺术区

Good to know

  • Fly in and out of Beijing Capital (PEK). It suits both the city hotels and Universal.
  • The longest train leg is about 2 hours.
  • Big museums and the Forbidden City close on Mondays. This plan works around them.
  • Northern Shanxi stays mild in June, usually cooler than Beijing.
  • Install Amap, Didi and Dianping, and set up Alipay or WeChat Pay before you fly.
  • Carry passports: trains and many sights use real-name tickets.
  • Buffets and seafood places serve raw dishes; everything listed has plenty cooked.
  • No public holidays overlap: Dragon Boat is 9 June 2027 and school holidays start 6 July.

  2. Forbidden City tickets on release (real-name; they sell out)
  3. Universal hotel and park tickets together (park day Sunday 20 June)

  8. Red Theatre acrobatics on Damai (the later show), then Da Dong for about 19:00
