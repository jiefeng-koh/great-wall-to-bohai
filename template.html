Day2
Monday 14 JuneToday

Mutianyu Great Wall

Monday is the quietest day on the Wall, and the Forbidden City is closed anyway. About 1.5 hours by car each way.

{{fig:mutianyu-tower|A watchtower on the Mutianyu wall among spring trees|A Mutianyu watchtower}}{{fig:toboggan|The toboggan track running down through the trees from the Wall|The toboggan down}}
  1. Breakfast

    Hotel buffet at the Kerry

    Early start, so keep it easy.

  2. 08:00

    Mutianyu Great Wall 慕田峪长城

    Leave by 8:00. Cable car up, toboggan down.

    {{go:慕田峪长城|Mutianyu Great Wall}}
  3. Lunch

    Rainbow trout in Mutianyu village

    The village speciality, grilled or braised.

  4. Rest stop

    Mu·Cafe at the foot of the Wall

    Great Wall-themed cups and a view of the watchtowers. Mutianyu is chestnut country, so ask for anything chestnut.

  5. 15:00

    Back at the Kerry

    Nap and pool.

  6. Late p.m.

    Hopson One 朝阳合生汇

    About 10 minutes by taxi. The mall young Beijingers use: trend and toy shops, snacks on every floor, and a three-level kids' play area. Pick up Lingcha or Yeye Bu Pao Cha milk tea.

    {{go:朝阳合生汇|Hopson One, Chaoyang}}
  7. 17:00

    Hen Jiu Yi Qian, Hopson One branch 很久以前羊肉串 合生汇店

    The lamb-skewer chain, inside the mall you're already in. Hopson One is where Beijingers shop, not a tourist strip, and a Monday at 17:00 is about as quiet as it gets. If there's a wait, take a number on Dianping and go back to the toy shops.

    {{go:很久以前羊肉串 合生汇店|Hen Jiu Yi Qian, Hopson One}}

Nearby & plan B

  • Heavy rain on the WallSwap Day 2 and stay in the cityThe Wall is slippery and the toboggan closes in rain. Spend the day at Hopson One and the Kerry pool, and try the Wall another morning if a day frees up.
  • Lunch in the villageThe Schoolhouse at MutianyuA long-running restaurant in a converted village school, with Western and Chinese dishes.{{go:慕田峪村|Mutianyu village}}
  • Skewers queue too longZi Guang Yuan 紫光园Beijing halal chain: lamb and beef, no chilli. Pick the nearest branch.{{go:紫光园|Zi Guang Yuan}}

Day3

Old city and a sunset wall ride

Train in, lunch, a long nap, one temple, then the wall ride. One evening activity only, and back at the hotel by about 20:30.

{{fig:datong-wall-tower|A corner tower on Datong's restored Ming city wall|The wall you ride at sunset}}{{fig:huayan|A hall at Huayan Temple with a bare tree in the courtyard|Huayan Temple}}{{fig:nine-dragon|Glazed tile dragons on Datong's Nine-Dragon Wall|The Nine-Dragon Wall, if there's energy}}
  1. Breakfast

    Qingfeng Baozi 庆丰包子铺

    Beijing's classic steamed-bun chain, branches all over the CBD. Pork-and-scallion buns.

    {{go:庆丰包子铺|Qingfeng Steamed Buns}}
  2. Train

    Beijing → Datong, 1.5–2 h

    Check which Beijing station your ticket leaves from.

  3. Lunch

    Longjuxiang Shaomai, by the Drum Tower

    Datong's thin-skinned shaomai. Open through the afternoon, so a late lunch off the train works.

    {{go:大同鼓楼|Datong Drum Tower}}
  4. Afternoon

    Check in and nap at Sunyata

    This is the important bit: the ride runs past bedtime. Try sea-buckthorn juice, Datong's local drink.

    {{go:大同既下山酒店|Sunyata by Jixiashan, Datong}}
  5. 16:30

    Huayan Temple 华严寺

    In the old city. An hour is plenty.

    {{go:大同华严寺|Huayan Temple, Datong}}
  6. 17:30

    Zini 369

    Early dinner, before the ride.

  7. ~19:00

    Six-seat bike on the city wall, from the East Gate 和阳门

    Ride south along the wall top as the sun sets, then straight back to the hotel.

    {{go:大同古城墙和阳门|Datong city wall, East Gate}}

Nearby & plan B

  • Energy left after HuayanNine-Dragon Wall 九龙壁A few minutes' walk away; 15 minutes is enough.{{go:大同九龙壁|Nine-Dragon Wall, Datong}}
  • Kids still wide awake after the rideSouth Gate shows 永泰门Mulan and a Northern Wei welcome ceremony at the gate.{{go:大同古城墙永泰门|Datong city wall, South Gate}}

  • Too tired to rideWalk a short stretch of wall at the South GateTen minutes up and back.

Day5

Back to Beijing, into the quest world

The quest world, a snack stop and a bakery all sit on the drive from the north-side stations to the PuXuan, so nothing doubles back.

  1. Breakfast

    Laochai Xiaomian

    Opens 06:30. Knife-cut noodles before the train.

  2. Train

    Datong → Beijing

    Most trains arrive at Qinghe or Beijing North; check yours. Book a car for the afternoon: it keeps the bags while you play, then drives on to the PuXuan.

  3. Afternoon

    Together Fantasy City, Beitou Shopping Park 北投购物公园

    Beside the Olympic Park: an immortals-and-alchemy world with quests, codes and prizes. Bring non-slip socks. The mall is full of trend-toy shops.

    {{go:北投购物公园|Beitou Shopping Park}}
  4. Lunch

    In the same mall

    Pick on Dianping on the day.

  5. Rest stop

    Huguosi Snacks, Di'anmen 护国寺小吃 地安门店

    06:00–20:30. Low-sugar Beijing pastries and naipizi, a milk-skin yogurt.

    {{go:护国寺小吃地安门店|Huguosi Snacks, Di'anmen}}
  6. On the way

    Daoxiangcun Treasure-Hunt Store, Longfu Temple 稻香村 隆福寺

    Beijing pastries in novelty boxes. The last stop before the hotel.

    {{go:隆福寺稻香村|Daoxiangcun, Longfu Temple}}
  7. Dinner

    Zi Guang Yuan 紫光园

    A long day, so keep it simple: the Beijing halal chain at the branch nearest the PuXuan. Lamb, beef and sesame pancakes, no chilli. Or eat in at the hotel.

    {{go:紫光园|Zi Guang Yuan}}

Day6
Friday 18 JuneToday

The Forbidden City

Walk from the hotel. You enter from the south (Meridian Gate) and leave by the north, so the day flows one way. Bring passports: tickets are real-name.

{{fig:jingshan-view|The golden roofs of the Forbidden City seen from Jingshan hill|The view from Jingshan}}{{fig:corner-tower|A corner tower of the Forbidden City reflected in the moat|The Corner Tower, beside the café}}
  1. Breakfast

    At the PuXuan

    The palace opens at 8:30. The walk to the south entrance takes about 20 minutes.

  2. 08:30

    Forbidden City, Meridian Gate 故宫博物院 午门

    South to north through the palace.

    {{go:故宫博物院午门|Forbidden City, Meridian Gate}}
  3. Then

    Jingshan Park 景山公园

    Just outside the north exit. Climb for the view over the palace roofs.

    {{go:景山公园|Jingshan Park}}
  4. Rest stop

    Corner Tower Café 角楼咖啡

    Just west of the north exit (Shenwu Gate). Palace-themed drinks and the matcha-peach "Thousand Miles of Mountains and Rivers" roll.

    {{go:故宫角楼咖啡|Corner Tower Café}}
  5. Lunch

    Cuihualou, Wangfujing 萃华楼

    Shandong cooking since 1940. A short taxi from Jingshan.

    {{go:萃华楼 王府井|Cuihualou, Wangfujing}}
  6. After lunch

    Rest at the PuXuan

  7. Late p.m.

    Wangfujing Xiyue, B2 王府井喜悦

    Walk down Wangfujing to the south end. B2 has around 30 small anime and game shops: badges, plush and cards, mostly ¥15–30. Wuyutai jasmine-tea ice cream on the way.

    {{go:王府井喜悦|Wangfujing Xiyue}}
  8. Dinner

    Fu Chun Ju, at the PuXuan

    The hotel's Michelin-starred restaurant.

Nearby & plan B

{{fig:beihai|The White Dagoba on Jade Island above Beihai Lake|Beihai Park}}

A free morning, then one sweep: lunch and weiqi in the west, east to the Temple, the show next door, dinner on the way back.

{{fig:taoranting|People relaxing by the lake under willows in Taoranting Park, Beijing|Taoranting Park}}{{fig:temple-heaven|The Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests at the Temple of Heaven|The Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests}}{{fig:kungfu|Martial artists in a stage kung fu performance|The Legend of Kung Fu}}

    Slow breakfast at the PuXuan, then the pool

  2. 11:00

    Jubaoyuan, Niujie 聚宝源 牛街

  5. 15:00

    Temple of Heaven, West Gate 天坛公园 西门

Tianjin on the way to the sea

Tianjin is on the line to the coast, so the stop adds about an hour of train time. Arrive and leave from the same station, and leave the cases there in between.

{{fig:jianbing|A street vendor making jianbing guozi on a griddle in Tianjin|Morning jianbing}}{{fig:tianjin-eye|The Tianjin Eye Ferris wheel over the Hai River|The Tianjin Eye, if it rains}}{{fig:italian-town|Italian Style Town in Tianjin|Italian Style Town, if there's time}}
  1. Morning

    Beijing South → Tianjin Station 天津站, 30 min

    Book a train that ends at Tianjin Station, not Tianjin West or Tianjin South. Then book the afternoon train to Beidaihe from the same station.

    {{go:天津站|Tianjin Station}}
  2. On arrival

    Cases into left luggage at the station

    There are staffed storage counters around Tianjin Station (about ¥10 a case for the day in 2025–26; search 天津站 行李寄存 in Amap, or use the 途简单 mini-program in WeChat) and self-service lockers in the Tianjin Station metro. Keep one day bag.

  3. Breakfast

    Jianbing guozi from a street stall

    Tianjin's own breakfast crêpe, in its hometown.

  4. Late a.m.

    Wudadao by horse carriage 五大道

    About 10 minutes by taxi. Streets of old European villas. Add the Porcelain House 瓷房子 only if everyone has energy (check Monday opening).

    {{go:五大道|Wudadao, Tianjin}}
  5. Lunch

    Kiessling, Xiaobailou 起士林 小白楼

    Tianjin's oldest Western restaurant: pot-roasted beef, pork knuckle and chestnut cake.

    {{go:起士林 小白楼|Kiessling, Xiaobailou}}
  6. Rest stop

    Minyuan Square 民园广场

    The old stadium at the heart of Wudadao, with cafés around the track.

    {{go:民园广场|Minyuan Square}}
  7. Back at the station

    Collect the cases, buy Guifaxiang mahua 桂发祥十八街麻花

    Tianjin's famous twisted dough, sold at the station.

  8. Afternoon

    Tianjin → Beidaihe 北戴河站, about 1 h 20, then 40 min to Aranya

    {{go:北戴河站|Beidaihe Station}}
  9. Dinner

    Then the first festival evening if the kids are up for it; otherwise an early night by the sea.

Good to know

  • Fly in and out of Beijing Capital (PEK). It suits both the city hotels and Universal.
  • The longest train leg is about 2.5 hours.
  • Big museums and the Forbidden City close on Mondays. This plan works around them.
  • Northern Shanxi stays mild in June, usually cooler than Beijing.
  • Install Amap, Didi and Dianping, and set up Alipay or WeChat Pay before you fly.
  • Carry passports: trains and many sights use real-name tickets.
  • Buffets and seafood places serve raw dishes; everything listed has plenty cooked.
