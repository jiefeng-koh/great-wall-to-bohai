No bookings and it queues hard: phone 010-6505-7988 about 2 hours ahead for a number, or be there by 17:00.

Day2
Monday 14 JuneToday

Mutianyu Great Wall

Monday is the quietest day on the Wall, and the Forbidden City is closed anyway. About 1.5 hours by car each way.

{{fig:mutianyu-cablecar|Orange Mutianyu cable cars over green wooded hills|The cable car up}}{{fig:mutianyu-tower|A watchtower on the Mutianyu wall among spring trees|A Mutianyu watchtower}}{{fig:toboggan|The toboggan track running down through the trees from the Wall|The toboggan down}}{{fig:lamb-skewers|Lamb skewers smoking on a charcoal grill|Lamb skewers for dinner}}
  1. Breakfast

    Hotel buffet at the Kerry

    Early start, so keep it easy.

    {{go:北京嘉里大酒店|Kerry Hotel Beijing}}
  2. 08:00

    Mutianyu Great Wall 慕田峪长城

    Leave by 8:00. On a Monday morning there's hardly a queue. The toboggan starts at Tower 6, reached by the chairlift; the enclosed gondola goes to Tower 14. They are run by different companies and sold separately, so buy chairlift up + toboggan down, or ride the gondola up and walk 14 → 6 first. Children under the toboggan's height limit ride with an adult, one child per adult. Hats and water: there's little shade on the Wall.

    {{go:慕田峪长城|Mutianyu Great Wall}}
  3. Lunch

    Rainbow trout, Yisonglou 翼松楼餐厅

    The village speciality, grilled or braised, at the restaurant inside the scenic area. A ¥68 buffet runs 11:30–14:30, with trout and chestnut pork.

    {{go:慕田峪 翼松楼餐厅|Yisonglou Restaurant, Mutianyu}}
  4. Rest stop

    Mu·Cafe at the foot of the Wall

    Great Wall-themed cups and a view of the watchtowers. Chestnut drinks are an autumn special, so they may not be on in June. The newer Mutianyu Great Wall Café (慕田峪长城咖啡, by the north ticket check, opened August 2026) has cup ice cream for the kids.

    {{go:慕田峪长城|Mutianyu Great Wall}}
  5. 15:00

    Back at the Kerry

    Nap and pool.

    {{go:北京嘉里大酒店|Kerry Hotel Beijing}}
  6. Late p.m.

    Hopson One 朝阳合生汇

    About 10 minutes by taxi. The mall young Beijingers use: trend and toy shops, snacks on every floor, and a three-level kids' play area. Drinks at TEAMT 茶山季, its first shop in China (December 2025): 红透半边天, a hawthorn-and-lemon tea with no milk, and gelato for the kids (rice or lychee-rose).Xiaohongshu: 红透半边天 “酸甜比例好正，完全不齁”

    {{go:朝阳合生汇|Hopson One, Chaoyang}}
  7. 17:00

    Hen Jiu Yi Qian, Hopson One branch 很久以前羊肉串 合生汇店

    The lamb-skewer chain, on B2 of the mall you're already in; about ¥90 a head. A Monday at 17:00 is about as quiet as it gets; take a number in its WeChat and go back to the toy shops. Ask for 原味, 不辣 for the kids. Skip the cold noodles.Xiaohongshu: service “比海底捞服务好”, a little pricey

    {{go:很久以前羊肉串 合生汇店|Hen Jiu Yi Qian, Hopson One}}

Nearby & plan B

  • Heavy rain on the WallSwap Day 2 and stay in the cityThe Wall is slippery and the toboggan closes in rain. Spend the day at Hopson One and the Kerry pool, and try the Wall another morning if a day frees up.
  • Lunch in the villageBeigala hotpot or Xiangwai Youyu 北旮旯涮肉 · 向外有鱼Two village restaurants outside the gate, if Yisonglou is busy: copper-pot lamb, or trout.{{go:慕田峪村|Mutianyu village}}

A Tongzhou mall, then over to Universal

The cases go to the Universal hotel first. Then a short taxi to Tongzhou Wanda, the everyday mall in Tongzhou's old centre, and back to the hotel for check-in.

{{fig:citywalk|Overview of Universal CityWalk Beijing|Universal CityWalk, this evening}}

    Beijing's classic steamed-bun chain; branches open 6:00–6:30. Find the nearest one on Amap the night before. Pork-and-scallion buns for everyone. Or the Kerry buffet.

    {{go:庆丰包子铺|Qingfeng Steamed Buns}}

    Kerry → Universal hotel with the cases, about 40 minutes

    A Didi XL fits the cases. The hotel holds them until check-in; keep a day bag.

  3. 10:00

    Tongzhou Wanda Plaza 通州万达广场

    About 15 minutes by taxi. An ordinary neighbourhood mall where Tongzhou families shop; about 3 hours is enough. For the kids: the B.Duck City Park (小黄鸭城市乐园) with a carousel and small rides, outdoors and best for the 8-year-old; the 宝贝王 arcade (2F), with good racing simulators (skip the claw machines). Shops: a DJI store to try drones and cameras, the Sisyphe bookshop (2F), The Green Party for cheap toys, stationery and blind boxes (2F), and 鹤所 low-sugar cakes. Lunch at 胖永辉, a Yonghui supermarket remodelled the Pang Dong Lai way (reopened May 2025): fresh bread, sliced braised beef and tastings. Grab 老北京酸奶 in clay jars and 北冰洋 from the chillers. Or out of the side door, 万达金街 (Wanda Golden Street) has snack stalls and milk tea.Xiaohongshu: “通州万达+金街从玩乐到美食、市井烟火，收获满满”, 161 likes

Day4
Wednesday 16 JuneToday

Universal Beijing Resort

Wednesday has the lowest average crowd level of the week here (queue-times.com, 2025–26). Early entry still matters.

{{fig:universal-panda|Kung Fu Panda Land lit up at night|Kung Fu Panda Land}}{{fig:universal-jurassic|Jurassic World Isla Nublar entrance arch at Universal Beijing|Jurassic World}}{{fig:universal-optimus|A giant Optimus Prime statue at Transformers Metrobase|Transformers Metrobase}}{{fig:universal-hogwarts|Hogwarts Castle spires at Universal Studios Beijing|Hogwarts Castle, for the Butterbeer}}
  1. Early

    Breakfast before the gate

    No source confirms a hotel breakfast box, so ask at check-in; otherwise eat the Peet's pastries from last night. Be at security before 7:00 for hotel-guest early entry. The first families arrive about 5:30.

    {{go:北京环球影城大酒店|Universal Studios Grand Hotel}}
  2. Opening

    Universal Beijing Resort 北京环球度假区

    One family did 12 rides between 7:30 and 12:30 with 5–30 minute waits. Ask at check-in which lands open for early entry: a Trip.com review from October 2026 says only Minion Land and Harry Potter, with the rest from 10:00. Do those first, then Kung Fu Panda, Transformers and Jurassic World. The Decepticoaster doesn't open early. Refill bottles at the free water points.

    {{go:北京环球影城|Universal Studios Beijing}}
  3. Lunch

    Mr Ping's Noodle House, Kung Fu Panda Land

    Po's dad's noodles, about ¥60–70 a dish. The dandan noodles and 口水鸡 are chilli dishes, so those are for the adults. Kids: beef noodles (牛肉拉面) or 炸酱面, with pork-and-corn fried dumplings (煎饺).

    {{go:北京环球影城 功夫熊猫盖世之地|Kung Fu Panda Land, Universal Beijing}}
  4. 12:30

    Back to the hotel for the heat

    12:30–15:30 is the hottest part of the day (32–35 °C). Get your hand stamped or ask at the gate about re-entry, then pool and nap. Back in for the late afternoon and the lights.

    {{go:北京环球影城大酒店|Universal Studios Grand Hotel}}
  5. 16:00

    Butterbeer in Hogsmeade

    ¥50 a cup, also frozen; buy it at a cart, not inside Three Broomsticks. And the Minion ice-cream bar in Minion Land, about ¥40. Skip a meal at Three Broomsticks: many 2026 "难吃" notes.

    {{go:北京环球影城 哈利波特的魔法世界|Wizarding World, Universal Beijing}}
  6. 17:30

    Toothsome Chocolate Emporium, CityWalk

    Steampunk dining room, giant milkshakes, and characters who come to the table. Budget about ¥200 a head: share one or two shakes, and burgers or wings for the kids. Cheaper treat next door: the Mengniu ice-cream and milk workshop (蒙牛冰淇淋&牛奶工坊).

    {{go:北京环球城市大道|Universal CityWalk Beijing}}
  7. Night

    Pack for the move

    Tomorrow everything goes by car to the Ascott, which has a washer-dryer.

Day5
Thursday 17 JuneToday

The Film Museum, 798 and Sanlitun

Everything goes by car to the Ascott first, so you see the museum and 798 with empty hands. They are 5 km apart.

{{fig:798|A gallery inside a former factory hall with arched roof at 798|Factory halls turned galleries}}{{fig:sanlitun|The central square of Sanlitun Taikoo Li South|Sanlitun Taikoo Li South}}
  1. Breakfast

    At the hotel or on CityWalk, no rush

    Peet's (皮爷咖啡) does a ham-and-egg panini set. Eat well: there's little food inside the museum.

    {{go:北京环球影城大酒店|Universal Studios Grand Hotel}}
  2. 10:00

    Universal → Ascott, about 40 minutes

    A Didi XL with all the cases. Check-in is from 15:00, so the front desk holds the bags.

    {{go:北京雅诗阁来福士中心服务公寓|Ascott Raffles City Beijing}}
  3. 11:00

    China Film Museum 中国电影博物馆

    About 30 minutes by taxi. Free, but book on the 影博之友 WeChat account 1–5 days ahead (参观预约, not 观影预约); under-6s don't need a booking. Closed on Mondays. Go to the 4th floor first: live wind, rain and thunder effects, green-screen flying and cartoon dubbing, and this area closes at 16:00. Then the 24 m dome screen and interactive floor on 1F, the old-movie street in Hall 12, and the Wandering Earth space suits on 2F–3F. Allow about 2.5 hours; little food inside, so bring real snacks.Xiaohongshu: “遛娃 给到夯” (top marks for kids), Aug 2026

    {{go:中国电影博物馆|China Film Museum}}
  4. 13:45

    Chichang, 798 吃厂

    About 10 minutes by taxi from the museum. A late lunch: tofu-pudding rice noodles (豆花米线, there's a mild one for kids) and green-plum ribs (青梅排骨).

    {{go:吃厂 798|Chichang, 798}}
  5. 14:45

    798 Art District 798艺术区

    Keep it to about an hour, mostly indoors: UCCA and one or two design shops, with a look at the outdoor installations on the way. A weekday is far quieter than the weekend.

    {{go:798艺术区|798 Art District}}
  6. 16:00

    Check in at the Ascott and rest

    About 20 minutes from 798. First load into the washer-dryer, and the kids' playroom. Rest until dinner.

    {{go:北京雅诗阁来福士中心服务公寓|Ascott Raffles City Beijing}}
  7. Optional

    Sanlitun Taikoo Li South 三里屯太古里南区

    Only if everyone still has energy. Trend shops and mainstream brands, 1.8 km from the Ascott; skip the luxury North side. Drinks: 阿嬷手作, Beijing's first shop, still has long queues in 2026, so order ahead in its mini-program (老椰清补凉 or 打·米麻薯, about ¥20–30).Xiaohongshu: still “三里屯排队王”, June 2026

    {{go:三里屯太古里南区|Sanlitun Taikoo Li South}}
  8. Dinner

    Xiaodiao Litang, Sanlitun flagship 小吊梨汤

    In Taikoo Li, opened September 2026. The opera stage runs at weekends only, so not on a Thursday. Pear soup poured from copper pots and the milk "cheese fish" (干酪鱼), the chain's best dish. For dessert, its 糖水铺: 杏仁豆腐, 豌豆黄 and 芸豆卷. Reviews of the chain are average, so go for the fun, not the food.

    {{go:小吊梨汤 三里屯|Xiaodiao Litang, Sanlitun}}

A day trip to Tianjin

30 minutes each way by train, no luggage, back at the Ascott by about 18:30. Most of it is outdoors, so pace it.

{{fig:jianbing|A jianbing being folded on a griddle|Jianbing in its home town}}{{fig:porcelain-house|The Porcelain House in Tianjin covered in shards of china|The Porcelain House}}{{fig:italian-town|Italian-style villas and a square in Tianjin|Italian Style Town}}{{fig:tianjin-eye|The Tianjin Eye ferris wheel over the Haihe river|The Tianjin Eye}}
  1. 08:10

    Ascott → Beijing South → Tianjin Station 北京南站 → 天津站

    About 35 minutes by taxi to Beijing South, then a C-train of 30–37 minutes. In the 2025 timetable, C2017 leaves 09:11 and arrives 09:41; check the 2027 times on 12306. Book for Tianjin Station (天津站), not Tianjin West or South.

    {{go:北京南站|Beijing South Station}}{{go:天津站|Tianjin Station}}
  2. 09:50

    Jianbing guozi near Tianjin Station

    Optional. A stall in a residential block 5–10 minutes' walk from exit 6. Double egg with 果篦 (the crisp cracker), ¥7, and a huge portion. Mornings only; skip it if the queue is over 20 minutes. Order 果篦 or 薄脆, not 果子. One commenter found it ordinary.Xiaohongshu: “果蓖特别酥脆”, 1,030 likes

    {{go:天津站|Tianjin Station}}
  3. 10:30

    Wudadao 五大道

    About 10 minutes by taxi. Walk the leafy lanes of 1920s villas to Minyuan Square (民园广场), the old stadium at the centre, with cafés around the track. Skip the horse carriage: ¥80 a person for a rushed 20 minutes, and one parent's kids preferred the boat.

    {{go:民园广场|Minyuan Square, Wudadao}}
  4. 11:30

    Er Duo Yan, Wudadao branch 耳朵眼会馆 五大道店

    57 Hubei Road (湖北路57号). Tianjin dishes in the former British consulate; lunch 11:00–14:00, about ¥280–300 a head. Halal, so no pork. Order 八珍豆腐 (“夯爆了”), 津味烧卖, 牛尾骨 and one 耳朵眼炸糕 each, the fried sweet-bean cake the brand is named for; skip 羊鲍鱼, which a newer review found gluey. Private rooms book on Dianping; the hall is walk-in. 10% service charge. Skip 狗不理 buns anywhere in Tianjin: years of "expensive and bad" reviews.

    {{go:耳朵眼会馆 五大道店|Er Duo Yan, Wudadao}}
  5. Optional

    Porcelain House 瓷房子

    A French villa covered in broken china, near Wudadao. Look from the taxi window on the way to Italian Town; no need to stop.

    {{go:瓷房子|Porcelain House, Tianjin}}
  6. 13:45

    Italian Style Town 意式风情区

    Squares and villas from the old Italian concession, across the river from Tianjin Station. About 30 minutes is enough in the heat.

    {{go:意式风情区|Italian Style Town, Tianjin}}
  7. 14:30

    Tianjin Eye 天津之眼

    A ferris wheel built over a bridge on the Haihe, with enclosed cabins, so fine in rain or heat. About 30 minutes round. Kids' drink: 海河牛奶, Tianjin's own flavoured milk (strawberry, chocolate, banana), from any convenience store.

    {{go:天津之眼|Tianjin Eye}}
  8. 16:00

    Guifaxiang mahua, a company-owned shop 桂发祥十八街麻花 直营店

    Buy only at a 直营店 (company-owned shop), where small packs are ¥36–40 a jin. No company-owned shop is confirmed near the Tianjin Eye or the station, and the Wudadao shop is only licensed. Ask "是直营店吗?" and check the price; the sure one is the main shop at 566 Dagu South Road (大沽南路566号). If it doesn't fit, skip it. Franchises, the Jinjie shop and the station kiosks charge ¥58 or more.

    {{go:桂发祥十八街麻花 大沽南路总店|Guifaxiang Mahua, Dagu South Road main shop}}

    Tianjin Station → Beijing South

    About 17:20 (C2020 or C2586 in the 2025 timetable). Then a taxi to the Ascott. Dinner close by: Guijie or the Raffles City food court (B1).

    {{go:天津站|Tianjin Station}}{{go:簋街|Guijie, Dongzhimen}}

The Forbidden City

Walk from the hotel. You enter from the south (Meridian Gate) and leave by the north, so the day flows one way. Bring passports: tickets are real-name.

{{fig:meridian-gate|The Meridian Gate of the Forbidden City across the stone forecourt|In by the Meridian Gate}}{{fig:kunning-palace|Palace of Earthly Tranquility in the Forbidden City|Kunning Palace, where tea is served}}{{fig:jingshan-view|The golden roofs of the Forbidden City seen from Jingshan hill|The view from Jingshan}}{{fig:wangfujing|Crowds walking down the Wangfujing pedestrian street|Down Wangfujing to Xiyue}}

    At the Ascott

    Check whether your rate includes breakfast. The palace opens at 8:30: leave by 7:50 and take a taxi to Tiananmen East (天安门东, about 20 minutes), then walk in to the Meridian Gate.

Good to know

  • Fly in and out of Beijing Capital (PEK). It suits both the city hotels and Universal.
  • The longest train leg is about 2 hours.
  • Big museums and the Forbidden City close on Mondays. This plan works around them.
  • Northern Shanxi stays mild in June, usually cooler than Beijing.
  • Install Amap, Didi and Dianping, and set up Alipay or WeChat Pay before you fly.
  • Carry passports: trains and many sights use real-name tickets.
  • Buffets and seafood places serve raw dishes; everything listed has plenty cooked.
  • No public holidays overlap: Dragon Boat is 9 June 2027 and school holidays start 6 July.
  • Universal is on a Wednesday: the quietest weekday on 2025–26 crowd data, and late June gets busier than mid-June.
