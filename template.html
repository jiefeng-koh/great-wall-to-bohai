13 – 26 June 2027 · a family journey

From the Great Wall to the Bohai Sea

Fourteen unhurried days through Beijing, Datong, Tianjin and Aranya — linked entirely by high-speed rail.

Mutianyu · Velatrix, CC0

The route

Four cities, one rail line at a time

West to Datong's walls and grottoes, back to the palaces of Beijing with a day by the river in Tianjin, east to the sea at Aranya, and a finale at Universal.

What you'll experience

The moments this trip is built around — each one chosen to work for adults and children alike.

{{fig:datong-wall-tower|A corner tower on Datong's restored Ming city wall}}Datong

Ride a 600-year-old city wall at sunset

A six-seat family bike along the 7.2 km wall top, ending at the South Gate for a Mulan performance.

{{fig:daiwangfu|Glazed dragon screen and stone lion at the gate of Daiwangfu, Datong}}Datong

A night in costume at a Ming palace

Hanfu dress-up and golden-hour photos at Daiwangfu, followed by its evening show.

{{fig:tai-chi|Locals practising tai chi under the trees in a Beijing park}}Beijing

Weiqi with Beijing's grandfathers

Walk-up games of go under the trees at Taoranting Park, where children and elders play each other.

{{fig:kungfu|Martial artists in a stage kung fu performance}}Beijing

The Legend of Kung Fu

A stage story of a boy who trains to become a master — martial arts, acrobatics and music, no Mandarin needed.

{{fig:beidaihe-dusk|Pink evening sky over the Bohai Sea near Beidaihe}}Aranya

A seaside theatre festival

Outdoor performances, parades and installations around the Lonely Library on the shore of the Bohai Sea.

{{fig:universal-panda|Kung Fu Panda Land lit up at night, Universal Beijing}}Beijing

Universal to finish

Stay at the park gate for early entry — Kung Fu Panda Land, Transformers and Jurassic World.

Where you'll stay

Five hotels, five different experiences — family comfort, design, heritage and the sea.

Beijing · 2 nights

Kerry Hotel

A huge kids' adventure zone and themed family rooms with their own slide.

Datong · 2 nights

Sunyata by Jixiashan

An Eastern-aesthetic design hotel in the old quarter, facing the city wall.

Beijing · 5 nights

The PuXuan

Ole Scheeren architecture, Michelin dining, and a short walk to the Forbidden City.

Aranya · 2 nights

Aranya Lanxi

Opened 2025: sea-view rooms, a private beach section and an infinity pool.

Universal Beijing · 2 nights

Universal Studios Grand Hotel

A themed hotel at the park gate, with early park entry for guests.

Chapter one · Days 1–2

Beijing: arrival and the Great Wall

Two easy days to land, let the children loose in a hotel built for them, and walk the Wall on its quietest day of the week.

Stay: Kerry Hotel Beijing, Chaoyang — 13–15 June

Day1
Sunday 13 June

Arrive in Chaoyang

A gentle first afternoon close to the hotel.

Do
Check in and let the kids explore the hotel's adventure zone.
Shop
Chaoyang Joy City — Pop Mart, trading-card and collectible shops.
Drink
Lingcha and Yeye Bu Pao Cha, two mainland-only tea brands you won't find at home.
Eat
Hai Tian Ge, in the hotel.
Day2
Monday 14 June

Mutianyu Great Wall

Monday is the quietest day on the Wall — and the Forbidden City is closed anyway.

Do
Cable car up, toboggan down.
Drink
Chestnut drinks at Great Wall Coffee by the north ticket gate.
Eat
Rainbow trout in Mutianyu village for lunch. Dinner at Zi Guang Yuan (halal, since 1912) or Amo at Hopson One.

Chapter two · Days 3–4

Datong: walls, grottoes and palace nights

An old imperial capital two hours west of Beijing, cooler in June, with the finest intact city wall you can ride on.

Stay: Sunyata by Jixiashan, old city — 15–17 June

Day3
Tuesday 15 June

Old city and a sunset wall ride

High-speed train from Beijing, about 1.5–2 hours. Big bags stay at the Kerry.

Eat
Longjuxiang Shaomai by the Drum Tower for lunch. Early dinner at Zini 369.
See
Huayan Temple and the glazed Nine-Dragon Wall.
Do
Around 7pm, rent a six-seat bike at the East Gate and ride south along the wall as the sun sets.
Watch
South Gate performances — Mulan and a Northern Wei welcome ceremony.
Drink
Sea-buckthorn juice, Datong's local drink.
{{fig:nine-dragon|Glazed tile dragons on Datong's Nine-Dragon Wall|The Nine-Dragon Wall, glazed in 1392}} {{fig:huayan|A hall at Huayan Temple with a bare tree in the courtyard|Huayan Temple}}
Day4
Wednesday 16 June

Yungang Grottoes and a palace in costume

An early start at one of Asia's great Buddhist sites, a rest, then a themed evening.

See
Yungang Grottoes at 7:30 opening — hunt for the tiny Buddhas and try stone rubbing.
Eat
Fenglinge for lunch (sweet-and-sour meatballs, shaomai). Dinner at Tianyuan Beiwei Jiayan.
Do
Hanfu costume and golden-hour photos at Daiwangfu, the Ming prince's palace.
Watch
Daiwangfu's evening show, Tianxia Datong.

Chapter three · Days 5–9

Imperial and creative Beijing

Five nights in the heart of the old city: palaces and temples, a hot-spring day, an immersive quest world, art, go and kung fu.

Stay: The PuXuan, Dongcheng — 17–22 June

Day5
Thursday 17 June

Back to Beijing, into the hot springs

Eat
Breakfast noodles at Laochai Xiaomian before the train.
Do
Jiuhao Hot Spring — one ticket covers the pools, buffet meals and an all-day kids' play area. Bring swimwear.
Day6
Friday 18 June

The Forbidden City

A weekday visit, walking straight from the hotel.

See
The palace from south to north, then the view over its roofs from Jingshan.
Drink
Palace-themed drinks at Corner Tower Café; Wuyutai jasmine-tea ice cream.
Eat
Cuihualou (Shandong cuisine, since 1940) for lunch. Dinner at Fu Chun Ju, the hotel's Michelin-starred restaurant.
Shop
Daoxiangcun Treasure-Hunt Store at Longfu Temple.

Saturday 19 June

798 Art District and Sanlitun

See
Immersive shows at Meet Museum, UCCA, and independent design shops.
Eat
Cantonese claypot rice at Kuanfan. Dinner on Guijie — Hu Da and crayfish (order some mild).

Day8
Sunday 20 June

Temple of Heaven, weiqi and kung fu

Old Beijing at its liveliest on a Sunday.

See
Temple of Heaven early, when locals do tai chi and fly kites.
Eat

Monday 21 June

Day trip to Tianjin

Thirty minutes by train, while Beijing's museums take their Monday off.

Eat

Aranya: art by the sea

A seaside community known for its architecture — timed for its summer theatre festival, when performances spill onto the beach.

Stay: Aranya Lanxi — 22–24 June

Day10
Tuesday 22 June

A quest world, then the coast

Do
Together Fantasy City — an immersive immortals-and-alchemy world with quests, codes and prizes. Bring non-slip socks.
Eat
Xiaodiao Litang in the same mall for lunch. Dinner at Changji Seafood in Aranya.
Travel
Afternoon train from Beijing Chaoyang, about 2.5 hours plus a 40-minute transfer.
Watch
The first festival evening, then Breeze Bar by the seaside auditorium.
Day11
Wednesday 23 June

Library, chapel and festival

See
The Lonely Library and Seashore Chapel in morning light, then UCCA Dune, a gallery set into the sand.
Eat
NINER'S American diner for lunch. Flower crab at Wuji Yugang for dinner, and late-night instant noodles at Suka — an Aranya ritual.
Shop
XXSHOKU bakery — egg tarts and pineapple cakes.
Watch
Outdoor festival performances, parades and street installations.

Good to know

  • Fly in and out of Beijing Capital (PEK) — it suits both the city hotels and Universal.
  • Every leg is by high-speed train; the longest is about 2.5 hours.
  • Beijing's big museums and the Forbidden City close on Mondays — this plan works around them.
  • Northern Shanxi stays mild in June, usually well below Beijing's temperatures.

Book early

  1. Aranya rooms, once the festival dates are announced
  2. Forbidden City tickets on release (real-name, they sell out)
  3. Universal hotel and park tickets together
  4. Yungang Grottoes time slot
  5. Kung fu show seats
