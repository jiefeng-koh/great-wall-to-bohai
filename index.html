Good to know

  • Fly in and out of Beijing Capital (PEK). It suits both the city hotels and Universal.
  • The longest train leg is about 2 hours.
  • Big museums and the Forbidden City close on Mondays. This plan works around them.
  • Northern Shanxi stays mild in June, usually cooler than Beijing.
  • Install Amap, Didi and Dianping, and set up Alipay or WeChat Pay before you fly.
  • Carry passports: trains and many sights use real-name tickets.
  • Buffets and seafood places serve raw dishes; everything listed has plenty cooked.
  • No public holidays overlap: Dragon Boat is 9 June 2027 and school holidays start 6 July.
  • Universal is on a Wednesday: the quietest weekday on 2025–26 crowd data, and late June gets busier than mid-June.
