Day1

Arrive in Chaoyang

Nothing booked except dinner, so a late landing costs nothing.

  1. Arrive

    Check in at the Kerry 北京嘉里大酒店

    Let the kids loose in the hotel's adventure zone and pool.

    AmapApple Maps
  2. Dinner

    Baoyuan Jiaozi Wu 宝源饺子屋

    About 15 minutes by taxi on Maizidian. A Michelin Bib Gourmand dumpling house whose wrappers are coloured with fruit and vegetables. Order pork and celery in rainbow colours.

    AmapApple Maps

SleepKerry Hotel Beijing

Day2
Monday 14 JuneToday

Mutianyu Great Wall

Monday is the quietest day on the Wall, and the Forbidden City is closed anyway. About 1.5 hours by car each way.

  1. Breakfast

    Hotel buffet at the Kerry

    Early start, so keep it easy.

  2. 08:00

    Mutianyu Great Wall 慕田峪长城

    Leave by 8:00. Cable car up, toboggan down.

    AmapApple Maps
  3. Lunch

    Rainbow trout in Mutianyu village

    The village speciality, grilled or braised.

  4. Rest stop

    Mu·Cafe at the foot of the Wall

    Great Wall-themed cups and a view of the watchtowers. Mutianyu is chestnut country, so ask for anything chestnut.

  5. 15:00

    Back at the Kerry

    Nap and pool.

  6. Late p.m.

    Hopson One 朝阳合生汇

    About 10 minutes by taxi. The mall young Beijingers use: trend and toy shops, snacks on every floor, and a three-level kids' play area. Pick up Lingcha or Yeye Bu Pao Cha milk tea.

    AmapApple Maps
  7. Dinner

    Hopson One's restaurant floors, or Zi Guang Yuan 紫光园

    Zi Guang Yuan is a Beijing halal chain: lamb and beef, no chilli. Pick the nearest branch.

    AmapApple Maps

Nearby & plan B

  • Heavy rain on the WallSwap Day 2 and stay in the cityThe Wall is slippery and the toboggan closes in rain. Spend the day at Hopson One and the Kerry pool, and try the Wall another morning if a day frees up.
  • Lunch in the villageThe Schoolhouse at MutianyuA long-running restaurant in a converted village school, with Western and Chinese dishes.AmapApple Maps
  • Duck instead of halalSiji Minfu 四季民福烤鸭店Popular roast-duck chain with several Chaoyang branches. Expect a queue; take a number on the app.AmapApple Maps

Datong: walls, grottoes and palace nights

Sleep: Sunyata by Jixiashan, old city · 15–17 June. Big bags stay at the Kerry.

Day3
Tuesday 15 JuneToday

Old city and a sunset wall ride

High-speed train from Beijing, about 1.5–2 hours. The wall ride runs past the kids' usual bedtime, so the afternoon nap matters.

  1. Breakfast

    Qingfeng Baozi 庆丰包子铺

    Beijing's classic steamed-bun chain, branches all over the CBD. Pork-and-scallion buns; adults can try chaogan, a garlicky braised liver stew.

    AmapApple Maps
  2. Train

    Beijing → Datong, 1.5–2 h

    Check which Beijing station your ticket leaves from.

  3. Lunch

    Longjuxiang Shaomai, by the Drum Tower

    Datong's thin-skinned shaomai. Open through the afternoon, so a late lunch off the train works.

    AmapApple Maps
  4. Afternoon

    Huayan Temple 华严寺 and the Nine-Dragon Wall 九龙壁

    Both in the old city, a short walk apart.

    AmapApple MapsAmapApple Maps
  5. Rest stop

    Nap and tea at Sunyata

    Try sea-buckthorn juice, Datong's local drink, sold all over the old city.

  6. Dinner

    Zini 369

    Early, before the ride.

  7. ~19:00

    Six-seat bike on the city wall, from the East Gate 和阳门

    Ride south along the wall top as the sun sets.

    AmapApple Maps
  8. Evening

    South Gate shows 永泰门

    Mulan and a Northern Wei welcome ceremony.

    AmapApple Maps

Nearby & plan B

  • Hot afternoon or rainDatong Museum 大同市博物馆Dinosaur fossils and Northern Wei treasures, indoors and air-conditioned. Closed Mondays, open Tuesday.AmapApple Maps
  • One more temple, if they're keenShanhua Temple 善化寺Quieter than Huayan, near the South Gate.AmapApple Maps
  • Too tired to rideWalk the wall at the South Gate insteadShorter, and you're already there for the show.

Day4
Wednesday 16 JuneToday

Yungang Grottoes and a palace in costume

An early start at one of Asia's great Buddhist sites, a long rest, then an early dinner before the evening.

  1. 06:45

    Breakfast at Sunyata

    Ask the night before for 6:45 breakfast or a breakfast box. Yungang is about 30 minutes by car.

  2. 07:30

    Yungang Grottoes 云冈石窟

    Be there at opening, before the heat and the tour groups. Hunt for the tiny Buddhas and try stone rubbing.

    AmapApple Maps
  3. Lunch

    Fenglinge 凤临阁

    11:00–14:00 in the old city. Sweet-and-sour meatballs and "hundred-flower" shaomai; knife-cut noodles in pork broth for the kids.

    AmapApple Maps
  4. Rest stop

    Afternoon at the hotel

    Snack: fried yellow-millet cakes (油炸糕) from an old-city stall.

  5. 17:00

    Tianyuan Beiwei Jiayan

    Early dinner at a well-reviewed local Shanxi kitchen.

  6. Golden hour

    Hanfu costume at Daiwangfu 代王府

    Dress up and take photos in the Ming prince's palace.

    AmapApple Maps
  7. Evening

    Tianxia Datong show at Daiwangfu

    Check the start time locally.

Nearby & plan B

  • A bigger day outHanging Temple 悬空寺A temple pinned to a cliff face, about 1.5 hours by car. Only if you swap out the afternoon rest.AmapApple Maps
  • Kids done with templesDatong Museum 大同市博物馆Dinosaur hall, indoors.AmapApple Maps

Sleep: The PuXuan, Dongcheng · 17–21 June

Day5
Thursday 17 JuneToday

Back to Beijing, into the quest world

The quest world, a snack stop and a bakery all sit on the drive from the north-side stations to the PuXuan, so nothing doubles back.

  1. Breakfast

    Laochai Xiaomian

    Opens 06:30. Knife-cut noodles before the train.

  2. Train

    Datong → Beijing

    Most trains arrive at Qinghe or Beijing North; check yours. Book a car for the afternoon: it keeps the bags while you play, then drives on to the PuXuan.

  3. Afternoon

    Together Fantasy City, Beitou Shopping Park 北投购物公园

    Beside the Olympic Park: an immortals-and-alchemy world with quests, codes and prizes. Bring non-slip socks. The mall is full of trend-toy shops.

    AmapApple Maps
  4. Lunch

    In the same mall

    Pick on Dianping on the day.

  5. Rest stop

    Huguosi Snacks, Di'anmen 护国寺小吃 地安门店

    06:00–20:30. Low-sugar Beijing pastries and naipizi, a milk-skin yogurt.

    AmapApple Maps
  6. On the way

    Daoxiangcun Treasure-Hunt Store, Longfu Temple 稻香村 隆福寺

    Beijing pastries in novelty boxes. The last stop before the hotel.

    AmapApple Maps
  7. Dinner

    Hen Jiu Yi Qian, Wangfujing APM 很久以前羊肉串

    A short walk from the PuXuan. The chain started in Beijing; close enough to come back another evening.

    AmapApple Maps

Nearby & plan B

  • Quest world sold out or closedChina Science and Technology Museum 中国科学技术馆In the same Olympic Park area. Hands-on halls and a children's science park. Closed Mondays; book a time slot.AmapApple Maps

Day6

The Forbidden City

Walk from the hotel. You enter from the south (Meridian Gate) and leave by the north, so the day flows one way. Bring passports: tickets are real-name.

  1. Breakfast

    At the PuXuan

    The palace opens at 8:30. The walk to the south entrance takes about 20 minutes.

  2. 08:30

    Forbidden City, Meridian Gate 故宫博物院 午门

    South to north through the palace.

    AmapApple Maps
  3. Then

    Jingshan Park 景山公园

    Just outside the north exit. Climb for the view over the palace roofs.

    AmapApple Maps
  4. Rest stop

    Corner Tower Café 角楼咖啡

    Just west of the north exit (Shenwu Gate). Palace-themed drinks and the matcha-peach "Thousand Miles of Mountains and Rivers" roll.

    AmapApple Maps
  5. Lunch

    Cuihualou, Wangfujing 萃华楼

    Shandong cooking since 1940. A short taxi from Jingshan.

    AmapApple Maps

    Rest at the PuXuan

    Wangfujing Xiyue, B2 王府井喜悦

    AmapApple Maps

    Fu Chun Ju, at the PuXuan

    The hotel's Michelin-starred restaurant.

Weiqi, the Temple of Heaven and kung fu

A free morning, then one sweep: lunch and weiqi in the west, east to the Temple, the show next door, dinner on the way back.

  2. 11:00

    Chengcha teahouse, inside Taoranting Park

  5. 15:00

    Temple of Heaven, West Gate 天坛公园 西门

    Weiduomei, Temple of Heaven bakery 味多美 天坛

    The Legend of Kung Fu, Red Theatre 红剧场

    AmapApple Maps

Good to know

  • Fly in and out of Beijing Capital (PEK). It suits both the city hotels and Universal.
  • The longest train leg is about 2.5 hours.
  • Big museums and the Forbidden City close on Mondays. This plan works around them.
  • Northern Shanxi stays mild in June, usually cooler than Beijing.
  • Install Amap, Didi and Dianping, and set up Alipay or WeChat Pay before you fly.
  • Carry passports: trains and many sights use real-name tickets.
  • Buffets and seafood places serve raw dishes; everything listed has plenty cooked.

Book early

  1. Aranya rooms, once the festival dates are announced
  2. Forbidden City tickets on release (real-name; they sell out)
  3. Universal hotel and park tickets together
  4. Yungang Grottoes time slot
  5. Kung fu show seats (the earlier show), then Da Dong for that evening
  6. Weekday tickets for Jiuhao Hot Spring and Together Fantasy City
  7. Car and driver for Tianjin (21 June) and the station pick-up on 23 June
